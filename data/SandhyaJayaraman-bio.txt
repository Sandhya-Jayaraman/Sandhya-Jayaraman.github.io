Sandhya Jayaraman is a PhD Candidate advised by Dr. Laurel Riek at the Healthcare Robotics Lab, UC San Diego. Her research takes a care ethics centered approach to the design, development, and deployment of robots for acute care. She closely works with disabled communities, patient populations, and inter-professional clinicians, centering their expertise and lived experiences in shaping technologies for acute care. Her work has been published at premier robotics venues including HRI, THRI, and CHB.
